# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves classmates, robotics researchers, mentors, potential collaborators, and recruiters who want to understand Leo Wei's background, projects, writing, and current interests from desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, personal record of Leo's robotics learning and project work. Visitors should quickly recognize who he is, inspect recent work and writing, and reach the relevant profile or contact channel without navigating a marketing funnel.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and curious. The experience should feel personal and technically capable, with restrained delight rather than corporate polish or theatrical spectacle.

## Anti-references

- A literal macOS desktop replica or a screenshot-like imitation of Finder.
- Decorative traffic-light controls, fake window chrome, or controls without a real action.
- Generic portfolio heroes, nested card grids, and marketing copy that overstates unfinished work.
- Colorful bubble canvases, ambient effects without purpose, or motion that delays repeated actions.
- Glassmorphism used as decoration without clear hierarchy or readable contrast.

## Design Principles

- Let real projects, notes, portraits, and contact paths carry the identity.
- Use the desktop composition as a spatial index while keeping every route direct and legible.
- Make motion explain hierarchy, continuity, and state; never make it a toll before content.
- Preserve a human, in-progress voice without weakening accuracy or professional credibility.
- Keep desktop, touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion experiences equally complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operability. Respect the user's reduced-motion preference, retain visible focus states, avoid color-only state communication, provide practical touch targets, and support Chinese-first content with English identity information where useful.
