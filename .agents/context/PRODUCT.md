# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Academic reviewers, graduate-school readers, internship recruiters, and collaborators who need a fast, credible read on Jiatong Wei's education, research direction, engineering evidence, and contact paths.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal academic and career card. It should help a visitor understand who Jiatong is, what fields he works in, which projects and awards support that claim, and where to inspect deeper notes or contact him.

## Brand Personality

Careful, technical, grounded. The site should feel like a compact research dossier rather than a marketing portfolio.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio templates, oversized hero sections, glassmorphism, decorative card grids, loud gradients, and visuals that make the beagle avatar feel more important than the academic identity.

## Design Principles

- Lead with verifiable identity and evidence.
- Keep the beagle as a small personal mark, not the page's main subject.
- Prefer restrained academic rhythm over promotional hierarchy.
- Let links, CV, projects, and wiki entries feel inspectable and practical.
- Use one memorable visual gesture at most, then keep the rest quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for primary text and controls, visible focus states, responsive layouts down to narrow mobile screens, and reduced-motion behavior for users who request it.
